---
description: Notes - Capture a thought into a well-filed note
argument-hint: what to capture
---

Take the raw thought below (or ask for it if nothing was given) and turn it
into a properly filed note:

1. If `$ARGUMENTS` is empty, ask what to capture before doing anything else.
2. Give the note a short, clear title and a descriptive kebab-case filename
   (e.g. `notes/client-x-pricing-idea.md`), dated if the content is
   time-sensitive (e.g. `notes/2026-07-19-standup-decision.md`).
3. Write the note: title, a short body capturing the thought in your own
   words (don't just paste the raw input back), and a date.
4. Check `notes/` for anything closely related and mention it: link to it
   if it's directly relevant, or note the overlap if it's a near-duplicate.
5. Confirm where the note was filed.

Default to `notes/` for anything that's a complete-enough thought to stand
on its own. If it's genuinely too raw to file yet, put it in `inbox/`
instead and say why.
