Search this workspace for: $ARGUMENTS

The point of a second brain is being able to get things back out, so bias
towards finding something useful over reporting a clean miss.

1. Search filenames first (`notes/`, `inbox/`, and anything else at the
   workspace root). A file whose name matches is usually the answer.
2. Then search file contents. Try the literal words, and also the obvious
   synonyms and related terms — someone looking for "the pricing decision" may
   have written "cost model" or "what we charge".
3. If a connected tool would plausibly hold it (email, calendar, Slack), say so
   and offer to sweep there too. Don't sweep unasked; it costs time.

Report what you found as a short list, newest first, each with:

- the file, as a clickable path
- one line on why it matched
- the sentence or two that actually answers the question, quoted

If nothing matches, say so plainly and suggest two or three different words to
try. Never pad the list with files that merely mention the word in passing —
a precise miss is more useful than a vague hit.
