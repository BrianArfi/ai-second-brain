---
description: Weekly - Reflect on the week - wins, open loops, themes
---

Look back over the last 7 days of activity in this workspace and write a
reflection:

1. Sweep `notes/` and `inbox/` for anything created or modified in the last
   7 days.
2. Draft a reflection with four sections:
   - **Wins**: what actually got finished or resolved
   - **Open loops**: things started but not closed out
   - **Themes**: patterns worth noticing across the week
   - **Next week**: what deserves priority based on the above
3. Save it to `notes/reflections/<YYYY>-W<ww>.md` (ISO week number), creating
   the `reflections/` folder if it doesn't exist yet.
4. Show the reflection after saving it.

Base every section on what's actually in the files; if the week was thin
on written material, say that plainly rather than padding the reflection.
